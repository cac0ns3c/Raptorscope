Velociraptor integration

The integration goes both ways

Ingest handles the built-in MacOS.System.* artifacts and real-world column drift — TCC's Allowed is the string "Yes"/"No" (not a bool) and ClientType is "Console"/"Service/Script" (not 0/1); both silently corrupted naive normalizers until reconciled against a live host.

A CI pairing guard enforces artifact↔detection coverage and rejects dead fields — you can't merge a capability without the detection that proves it fires.

Custom VQL, upstreamed

Where no adequate built-in exists, Raptorscope ships custom VQL — validated on a real Mac and submitted to the Artifact Exchange:

  • BTM — sfltool dumpbtm persistence (macOS 13+)
  • ConfigProfiles — profiles show (MDM/config profiles)
  • Netstat — sockets joined to the owning process
  • SignedProcesses — codesign trust, not just a hash
